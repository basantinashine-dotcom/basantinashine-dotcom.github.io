What the problem was, what I did, and what changed.

Search ads written for every keyword

Amazon

Amazon’s Google search ads used to come from generic category templates, so a specific search got a vague ad. I led ten engineers and four applied scientists to replace them with ads written for each keyword, grounded in Amazon’s own search results and product details. US click-through rose 11.75%, the biggest improvement any one product had driven.

The harder problem was trust. To run AI-written ads at that scale, we needed an evaluation system that judged them the way a person would. Ours agreed with human reviewers 99% of the time.

Remembering why a shopper came

Amazon

Shoppers often reach Amazon from an ad or a search result somewhere else. I wrote the case for carrying that outside context into Amazon’s ranking and recommendations, brought five organizations behind one roadmap, and launched the platform. Conversion from that traffic rose 16%, adding $430M in incremental revenue.

Agents that do the busywork for advertisers

Amazon

Seasonal campaigns take a lot of manual setup. My team built SmartCampaign, an AI agent that finds keywords, drafts headlines and analyzes performance, so advertisers can launch campaigns more often. Setup completion rose 34%, and manual setup time fell 63%.

Teams across Amazon Ads were each building their own agent plumbing, so I defined one shared framework with permissions, approvals and explanations of agent actions built in. 22 teams adopted it, saving 260 engineering weeks.

What should a marketer do next?

Orcana.AI

At Orcana.AI, I’m leading a Next Best Engagement agent that recommends a marketer’s next outreach across channels. In a pilot, it delivered twice the revenue lift of traditional rule-based systems. We use an LLM to review agent traces and find the failures that matter most, which has raised the task success rate by 15%.
