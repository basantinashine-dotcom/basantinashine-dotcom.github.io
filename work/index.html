Selected work

What the problem was, what I did, and what changed.

Search ads written for every keyword

Amazon

Amazon’s Google search ads used to come from generic category templates, so a specific search got a vague ad. I led ten engineers and four applied scientists to replace them with ads written for each keyword, grounded in Amazon’s own search results and product details. In a US A/B test, click-through rose 11.75%, the biggest improvement any one product had driven.

The harder problem was trust. To run AI-written ads across millions of ad variants, we needed an evaluation system that judged them the way a person would. Benchmarked on relevance, factual accuracy and policy compliance, it agreed with human-labeled examples 99% of the time.

Remembering why a shopper came

Amazon

Shoppers often reach Amazon from an ad or a search result somewhere else. I wrote the case for carrying that outside context into Amazon’s ranking and recommendations, won VP and SVP buy-in across five organizations, aligned 20+ partner teams behind one roadmap, and launched the platform. In A/B tests, conversion from that traffic rose 16%, adding $430M in incremental revenue.

Agents that do the busywork for advertisers

Amazon

Seasonal campaigns take a lot of manual setup. My team built SmartCampaign, an AI agent that finds keywords, drafts headlines and analyzes performance, so advertisers can launch campaigns more often. Setup completion rose 34%, and manual setup time fell 63%.

Teams across Amazon Ads were each building their own agent plumbing, so I defined one shared framework with permissions, approvals and explanations of agent actions built in. 22 teams adopted it, saving 260 engineering weeks.

Marketing answers in hours, not months

Orcana.AI

Marketing teams waited months for analysis to learn what was actually working. As Head of Product at Orcana.AI, I lead the team, roadmap and evaluation framework for our analytics agents, and I led the launch of a Marketing Mix agent that answers those questions directly, cutting analysis time from 12 weeks to 2 hours. We’re now live with four enterprise customers.

The architecture reflects a deliberate choice: an LLM interprets the question, and a proprietary deterministic analytics engine performs the analysis. Customers can ask questions in natural language without their data going to third-party models.

What should a marketer do next?

Orcana.AI

At Orcana.AI, I’m also leading a Next Best Engagement agent that recommends a marketer’s next outreach across channels. It’s in a pilot, separate from the launched Marketing Mix agent above. In the pilot, it delivered twice the revenue lift of traditional rule-based systems. We use an LLM to review agent traces and find the failures that matter most, which has raised the task success rate by 15%.
