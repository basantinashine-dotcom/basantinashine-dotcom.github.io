My Philosophy · A working manifesto

My product philosophy.

Great products live where three understandings overlap. Scroll, tap or hover to explore.

01 · Brainstorm

Understand customers

Everything starts with people. Listen, observe and sit with their problems. This is where ideas are born: generously, without judgment.

1

01 · Brainstorm

Understand customers

Everything starts with people. Listen, observe and sit with their problems. This is where ideas are born: generously, without judgment.

2

02 · Filter

Understand strategic goals

Not every good idea is the right idea. Strategic goals tell us which ideas move the business forward and which are distractions.

3

02 · Filter

Understand the market space

What already exists? Where are the gaps? The market shows where an idea can win and where it would simply blend in.

4

03 · The sweet spot

Ideas that survive both filters

What remains is small and sharp: ideas customers want, that serve the strategy, in a space we can win.

5

04 · Dive deep

Go back to customers

With the filtered ideas in hand, return to customers and dive deep. Refine the details with them. That second pass is how an experience becomes truly differentiated.

What happens when you skip a circle

Missing: market

Customers + strategy

Loved and on-strategy, but a me-too in a crowded space. Hard to win.

Missing: strategy

Customers + market

A nice feature in an open gap that goes nowhere for the business.

Missing: customers

Strategy + market

Looks great in a deck. Nobody actually asked for it.

Brainstorm wide. Filter hard. Dive deep.

Start with people. End with people.
